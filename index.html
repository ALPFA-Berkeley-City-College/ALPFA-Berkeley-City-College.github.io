Association of Latino Professionals For America

Build your career network. One lunch hour at a time.

ALPFA BCC is the Berkeley City College chapter of the Association of Latino Professionals For America. We help students build professional skills, meet working professionals and connect with chapters across the Bay Area. Membership is free, and our events fit into the BCC lunch hour.
